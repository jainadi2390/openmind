PROBABILITY THEORY TEST DOCUMENT

Black and White Balls Problem:
If you have a box containing 4 black balls and 6 white balls (total 10 balls), the probability of drawing a white ball is calculated as follows:

P(White) = Number of white balls / Total number of balls
P(White) = 6/10 = 0.6 or 60%

P(Black) = Number of black balls / Total number of balls
P(Black) = 4/10 = 0.4 or 40%

Important Notes:
- Total balls must equal the sum of all colored balls
- Probability must be between 0 and 1
- All probabilities must sum to 1

STATISTICS FORMULAS

Mean (Average):
Mean = Sum of all values / Number of values

Median:
The middle value when data is sorted
- For odd number of values: middle value
- For even number of values: average of two middle values

Mode:
The value that appears most frequently in the dataset

ADVANCED PROBABILITY

Conditional Probability:
P(A|B) = P(A and B) / P(B)

Independent Events:
P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)

Mutually Exclusive Events:
P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B)
